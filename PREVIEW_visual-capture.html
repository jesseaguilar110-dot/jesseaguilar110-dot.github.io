The fence

What this tier will not do, in plain terms.

There is no correction state, no ground control, no lidar and no verification. Nothing produced here carries a measurement claim, and none of it should be used to take a dimension, compute a volume, position an asset, or support a design or a submittal.

If it turns out you need any of that, the answer is not a discount on the verified tier. It is a different flight, on different equipment, with a control plan. We would rather tell you that during the quote than after delivery.

The reason we hold this line is the same reason the verified tier is worth anything. A firm that quietly lets visual work pass as measurable has no defensible product left to sell.
