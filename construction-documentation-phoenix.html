Why a dated record is worth paying for

Most disputes on a build come down to what the site looked like on a particular day, and most sites have nothing but phone photos to answer with. A controlled capture answers it with geometry, at a position anyone can check.

Conditions under a slab stop existing the moment it is poured. Captured beforehand, they stay available for the life of the asset.

Recurring visits are priced as a program rather than as a series of one-off flights, which is what makes a monthly cadence affordable.

Which tier a build needs

If anyone will measure from the result — quantities, elevations, positions — it is Verified Capture, controlled and independently validated. If the imagery is for leasing, updates and marketing, Visual Capture does the job at around half the cost. We will tell you which one applies, including when it is the cheaper one.
