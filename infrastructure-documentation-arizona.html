  • VERTICAL ASSETSTowers, monopoles and stacks documented from the air, producing an inventory and condition record of mounted equipment with no climb and no downtime.

A geospatial mapping product. Not a substitute for a structural assessment or for work requiring a licensed professional.

Multi-source capture, one record

Aerial photogrammetry covers the whole structure. Terrestrial LiDAR fills close-range detail on portions that can be reached on foot. Both land in a single georeferenced model, positioned with GNSS and subjected to independent quality-control checks before it is delivered.

Every delivery states what was not captured. An owner who is not told a space is unmapped will assume it was mapped, and that assumption is how bad records get made.
