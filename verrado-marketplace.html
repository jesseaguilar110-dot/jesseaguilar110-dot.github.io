Verrado Marketplace — a commercial build documented while it was still moving.

Controlled aerial mapping of an active retail development, delivered as a georeferenced 3D record of the site.

What we were documenting

Verrado Marketplace is a commercial retail development under construction in Buckeye, Maricopa County, a few miles from our door. A site in active construction changes weekly, and once concrete is poured the conditions underneath it are gone unless somebody recorded them. Axis Fields documented the development with controlled aerial mapping, producing a georeferenced 3D record of the site as it stood on the day of capture. The result is a spatial record the owner and design team can measure from, share with consultants, and compare against later capture — not a marketing flyover, and not a substitute for work that requires a licensed professional.

What the project required

Commercial construction produces decisions that get made off imagery, then defended years later. That raises the bar past a set of photographs: the record has to be positioned independently, so a dimension taken from it holds up, and it has to state plainly what it is and is not.

How Axis Fields approached it

Axis Fields combined controlled aerial capture with GNSS-positioned field observations to produce a georeferenced record of the site. Positioning was established independently rather than relying on the aircraft's own estimate, and the reconstruction was subjected to independent quality-control checks before delivery.

What was produced

  • Orthomosaic imagery of the development at a stated ground sample distance
  • Point cloud and textured 3D site model
  • Elevation and terrain models
  • GIS-ready deliverables in the formats the client's systems already read
  • A written record of correction state, reference frame and what was not captured

How the information can be used

As a dated baseline for later progress epochs, for earthwork volumes, for coordination between trades, and as a record of conditions that are no longer visible once the build advances.
