Property & land · West Valley and Metro Phoenix

Some properties cannot be photographed from the ground.

Acreage, vacant land and development parcels all have the same problem: standing at the gate tells a buyer almost nothing. From the air, the shape of the parcel, the access, the frontage and what surrounds it read in a single frame.

Request a quote

What this answers for a buyer

  • How big it actually is, and what shape it is
  • Where the access is and what the frontage looks like
  • What is next door, and what is coming next door
  • Terrain, drainage lines and vegetation across the whole parcel
  • How it sits relative to roads, utilities and development

What arrives

  • Edited aerial stills, sized for a listing and for print
  • Aerial video cut to length for the platform it is going on
  • Overhead parcel imagery, annotated when a parcel needs explaining
  • Ground and interior imagery when the property has structures

Who this is for

Agents and brokers listing land or acreage, owners marketing a parcel, and developers who need to show a site to partners who are not going to drive out to it.

Marketing imagery. Nothing on this page is a boundary determination, and nothing in a listing capture should be used to take a measurement. When a parcel needs measurable geometry, that is mapping work and we fly it differently.

Turnaround

Most listings do not have three weeks.

A single parcel is usually one visit and a short edit. Tell us the address and when it needs to be live, and the quote comes back with a delivery date on it.

Properties inside the Luke Air Force Base airspace, a large part of the West Valley, are schedulable on short notice here, which is not true of every operator in the area.
