Verified Capture

Corrected, controlled and independently graded. When somebody downstream is going to measure from the model, take a quantity off it, or design against it, this is the tier that survives the question of where the numbers came from.

Method

Accuracy is measured, not asserted.

Measured independently of the software that produced the model, and reported with the data. If that distinction means something to you, you already know why it matters.

  • CONTROLGNSS-positioned ground control established on site, independent of any third-party correction feed.
  • VERIFICATIONGraded against evidence the reconstruction never saw.
  • RECORDCorrection state, reference frame and epoch delivered with the data, so the result can be checked by whoever receives it.

The flight is evidence too

How a site is flown decides what the reconstruction can resolve, long before any processing happens. The capture is planned against the deliverable and the plan is retained with it, so a result can be traced back to the mission that produced it.

A capture that cannot be described step by step is a capture that cannot be repeated, and one that cannot be repeated cannot be defended.

Limits, stated up front

What Verified Capture is not.

It is a geospatial mapping product, not a regulated professional service. Axis Fields delivers measurable, independently validated spatial data. That is a narrower claim than the one a licensed professional makes, and we hold the line on it.

Where a project requires a stamped, certified product, that work belongs to a licensed professional. If your project already has one engaged, we coordinate directly and our data is theirs to check. Independent verification is welcome, not something we route around. Tell us early and the whole job costs less.

Photogrammetry and lidar do not see through walls. No interior access means interiors come back hollow. We say so in writing before collection, not after, because a responder who does not know a space is unmapped will assume it is mapped.

A datasheet figure is not a result. We will not quote a sensor's relative accuracy specification as though it were verified absolute accuracy achieved on your site. If you have been handed a number like that, ask what it was validated against.
