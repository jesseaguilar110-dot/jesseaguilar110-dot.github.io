Flying the Valley

Metro Phoenix is not open airspace. Sky Harbor sits in the middle of it, and Deer Valley, Chandler, Falcon Field, Goodyear, Glendale and Mesa Gateway ring the metro with controlled airspace of their own. Around Luke Air Force Base there is no automated authorization at all, which closes a large part of the northwest Valley to operators who have not arranged access in advance.

We resolve airspace before quoting rather than on the morning of the flight, which is why a date we give you is a date we keep. Sites inside the Luke area are schedulable on short notice under a standing FAA authorization we hold through 2028.

Season matters here too. Summer heat, monsoon wind and low winter sun all move the capture window, and the difference between a clean data set and a reflight is usually the hour of the day it was flown.
