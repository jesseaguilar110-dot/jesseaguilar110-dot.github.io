Airspace most operators cannot schedule into

A large share of the West Valley sits inside Luke Air Force Base's controlled airspace, covering parts of Glendale, Peoria, El Mirage, Surprise, Litchfield Park and the corridor out toward Waddell and Citrus Park. There is no automated approval path in that airspace. An operator without prior arrangement is looking at a request that takes weeks, per site.

Axis Fields holds a standing FAA authorization for that area, valid through September 2028. In practice that means a West Valley site can be scheduled on the same timeline as one in open airspace, and a date we quote is a date we can actually fly.

What the West Valley needs mapped

This is one of the fastest-building corridors in Maricopa County, and most of what we capture here is mid-construction. Sites change weekly, and conditions under a slab are gone once it is poured unless somebody recorded them.

  • Construction documentation on active commercial, industrial and residential builds
  • Development and land parcels captured before ground is broken
  • Infrastructure and asset documentation for owners and engineers
